Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Zoom Out is a daily photo guessing game at zoomout.dev and inside Discord. It's run by Nawar, an individual based in Switzerland. The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and nothing that identifies you personally is stored by us.

Questions or requests: hello@zoomout.dev

What stays on your device

Your results, streaks and settings are saved in your own browser (local storage), along with a random player ID. This never leaves your device unless described below. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for zoomout.dev.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). Like any web host, Vercel's servers process technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages, and may keep short-term logs for security and reliability. See Vercel's privacy policy. All fonts are served from zoomout.dev itself, not from Google or other third parties.

Visitor statistics

On the website, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies and no fingerprinting, and only shows us totals (such as page views and countries), never individual visitors. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Visitor statistics are not collected inside Discord.

Player statistics

To show how you compare ("faster than X% of players"), the result of the daily puzzle may be sent to our database at Supabase (servers in the EU, Frankfurt). Only these items are sent: the puzzle date, how many guesses you needed, whether you solved it, and your random player ID. No name, email, IP address or Discord account is stored with it. Results can only be read as totals.

Playing inside Discord

When you play Zoom Out as a Discord Activity, the game is loaded through Discord, and Discord processes your use of it under Discord's privacy policy. Zoom Out does not ask you to log in with Discord and does not receive your username, profile, messages or friends list. Discord passes technical IDs (such as the ID of the channel the Activity runs in) needed to run the game; we don't store them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No accounts, so there are no passwords or emails to leak.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for players in the EU, the GDPR, you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Because results are stored only with a random ID, we usually can't link them to you, but we'll help where we can. Write to hello@zoomout.dev. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local data protection authority.

Children

Zoom Out is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone. Inside Discord, Discord's own age requirements apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
