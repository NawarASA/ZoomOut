    How to play
    
    
    Every day there's one new mystery photo. It starts extremely zoomed in. Work out what it is.
    

    
      
      
    1. 
        
      Look closely
      
        
      The photo starts at 16× zoom, so at first it may just look like a texture or a colour.
      
      
      2. 
      
    2. 
        
      Make a guess
      
        
      Type what you think the object is, like tennis ball or key. Small typos are fine.
      
      
      3. 
      
    3. 
        
      Watch it zoom out
      
        
      Every wrong guess pulls the camera back one step and shows more of the photo. Stuck? Tap Zoom out to skip a guess.
      
      
      4. 
      
    4. 
        
      Solve it within six zooms
      
        
      
          
          16×12×8×5×2.5×1×
        
      
        
      The fewer zooms you need, the better your score.
      
      
      5. 
    
    

    
    What the colours mean
    
    
      
      
    • Got it. You solved it.
      • 
      
    • Close. You're on the right track, like golf ball for a tennis ball.
      • 
      
    • No. Not it this time.
      • 
      
    • Skipped. You zoomed out without guessing.
      • 
    
    

    
    A new photo arrives every day at midnight. When you're done, copy your result and see if your friends can beat it.
    
    
  
    

    


    
  
    
  
    
    
    
    Add to Home Screen
    
    
    Zoom Out works like an app on your iPhone. It takes two taps.
    
    
      
      
    1. 
        
      Tap the Share button
      
        
      It's the square with an arrow pointing up , at the bottom of Safari or next to the address bar.
      
      
      2. 
      
    2. 
        
      Choose "Add to Home Screen"
      
        
      Scroll down the menu if you don't see it, then tap Add.
      
      
      3. 
    
    
    
    Zoom Out then opens full-screen from its own icon, with a new photo every day.
